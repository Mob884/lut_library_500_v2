# Privacy Policy — LutPoint

_Last updated: 2026-10-14_

LutPoint ("the app") is a photo color-grading app. We built it to work with as little data collection as possible.

## What the app does with your photos
Photos you choose in the app are processed **entirely on your device**. They are never uploaded to any server we operate. When you save a graded photo, it is written directly to your device's photo library.

## What data leaves your device
- **LUT library:** the app downloads publicly available preset data (names, categories, preview images, `.cube` files) from our GitHub-hosted library. This is a one-way, anonymous download — no information about you or your device is sent with these requests.
- **In-app purchases:** purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment information. Google's handling of purchase data is governed by the [Google Play privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **App version check:** on launch, the app checks a small public file to see if an update is required. No personal data is included in this request.

## What we don't do
- We don't require an account or login.
- We don't collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports.
- We don't access your contacts, location, or microphone.
- We don't sell or share data with third parties, because we don't collect any.

## Permissions
- **Photo library access:** only to let you pick a photo to edit and save the result back to your library.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL.

## Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact details listed on the app's Play Store page.
